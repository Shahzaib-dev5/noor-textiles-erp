# Wholesale customer management - Phase 10

Open `/customers` for the shared customer directory. Create at `/customers/new`; profile, edit and statement URLs use `/customers/:id`, `/edit` and `/statement`. Existing customer IDs remain authoritative in core `customers`; optional `wholesale.customers` profiles extend them, including legacy customers edited explicitly. No customer is copied into a separate sales ledger.

Profiles include stable company-unique code, Urdu/English shop names, owner/contact, fictional phone references, email, city/market/address, primary group, payment terms/days, credit limit/hold/review notes, customer discount and notes. New opening balance is an explicit field of the original customer model. Once a customer exists, its opening balance cannot be silently edited. Display metadata may change; existing codes remain stable once the operational profile is established. Referenced customers are deactivated, never deleted. Inactive customers cannot submit/approve new orders or dispatch goods.

`/sales/customer-groups` supports wholesalers, distributors, retailers, resellers, commission agents and institutional buyers. Group defaults populate a new/changed profile in the form; saving is explicit. Existing customer-specific terms remain their recorded policy. The UI accepts discount percentages; stored calculations use integer basis points. Credit, discount and opening-balance changes require the simulated owner; the role switcher is not secure authorization.

Profiles show original invoices, allocated receipts, unallocated cash credit, running receivable statement, historical and operational orders/dispatches, outstanding receivable, remaining commitments, unbilled dispatches and credit headroom separately. A statement credits only actual invoice allocations; unallocated cash is shown separately. Orders and dispatches do not become receivables or cash entries.

All contacts are fictional demo references. Historical logistics fields remain explicitly unknown. The existing screen-manual PDF and generator are preserved; they describe their earlier capture and are not silently regenerated by Phase 10.
