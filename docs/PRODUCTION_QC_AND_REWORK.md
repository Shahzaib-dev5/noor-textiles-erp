# Production quality and rework

QC documents identify the original component lot, stage, current internal quality state, inspector and quantity. Accepted + held + rejected + rework must equal the inspection quantity. Nonaccepted outcomes require defect details. Posting transfers only existing internal components, with no new fabric or output creation.

An inspection100 =85 accepted +5 held +4 rejected +6 rework produces four exclusive balances. A rework request locks only available, unassigned REWORK components. It records correction, team, optional responsible contractor reference, due date and estimated cost. The contractor field alone does not transfer physical custody.

Rework moves OPEN → IN_PROGRESS → AWAITING_INSPECTION → COMPLETED. Submission alone does not accept components. A fresh inspection must reference the rework request and cannot exceed its unsettled quantity. Further rework requires correction and resubmission. Passing6 later changes accepted85 to91 without increasing original output100.

Open rework prevents readiness. Held/rejected components remain visible until inspected or explicitly disposed under owner permission. Posted inspections and disposals cannot be replayed. Rework cost is an estimate until a separately approved, uniquely referenced expense is recorded once for the completed request.
